Extra movement flag

Steps where operation is Premarshal ahead of retrieval or Digout to clear an overstow are extra movements — they are reshuffles/rehandles, not directly productive. Wherever these steps appear in task lists, operator timelines, or activity detail views, add a small visual indicator — a tag, dot, or subtle background tint — that distinguishes them from productive moves (Putaway, Outbound, Vessel Discharge).

If there's a dashboard or summary view, add a Rehandle Ratio metric: count of extra-movement steps divided by total steps, shown as a percentage. Label it "Rehandle Ratio" with a brief tooltip "Ratio of reshuffle/digout moves to total moves — lower is better".
